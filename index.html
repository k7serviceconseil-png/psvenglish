Instructions

Suggested duration: 60 minutes. This test is designed to be completed in one sitting, without interruption.

Rules:

  • The test contains 50 multiple-choice questions.
  • Each question offers 5 answer choices (A to E).
  • For some questions, more than one answer may be correct.
  • When the option "All of these answers are correct" (E) is offered, all applicable choices (A to D) must also be selected. Selecting only E does not provide full credit.
  • When the option "None of these answers" (E) is relevant, it is considered correct.
  • Read each question carefully before answering.
  • Select all answers you believe are accurate.
  • The questionnaire unlocks only after the timer has been started.
  • You may use a maximum of 3 pauses. Each pause is limited to 15 minutes.
  • During a pause, the questions are locked and cannot be changed.
  • The time used and the number of pauses will be submitted with the results.

General Guidelines:

  • The test is individual and must be completed without outside assistance.
  • Make sure your browser is stable and that you have a reliable Internet connection.

Purpose of the Test:

This questionnaire assesses your sales competencies across 13 key areas, including prospecting, needs discovery, objection handling, closing, follow-up, and more.

The results will be used to identify your strengths and development areas, so that the most relevant training or coaching priorities can be targeted.

Sales Success Profile® - Important Notice

The Sales Success Profile® was designed to provide valuable information about your sales skills and aptitudes. It helps identify your strengths and weaknesses across 13 key areas of the sales process, including:

  1. Approach and initial contact
  2. Needs discovery
  3. Solution presentation
  4. Objection handling
  5. Negotiation
  6. Closing the sale
  7. Follow-up and customer retention
  8. Time management
  9. Territory planning
  10. Prospecting
  11. Active listening
  12. Creativity and adaptability
  13. Professionalism

The results may be used by your managers or employer to improve your sales skills by targeting training in the areas where support is needed.

By completing this profile, you expressly agree that your answers may be evaluated and that the results may be shared with the company or agency that asked you to complete this profile.

The Sales Success Profile® measures sales competencies, not personality. Sales competencies can be trained and improved. However, it is essential to understand your current level in order to identify development priorities.

This is a 50-question multiple-choice test covering 13 critical competencies. Your answers will support a structured analysis of your strengths, development areas, and sales progression priorities.
